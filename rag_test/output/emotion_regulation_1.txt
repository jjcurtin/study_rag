# Stress-to-Choice Worksheet

Stress can bring strong feelings and thoughts about using. This worksheet helps you notice stress early and choose one safe action. You do not have to solve everything right now. Focus on the next small step. [1]

**Date:** ____________________  **Time:** ____________________

### 1. Name it
The main feeling I have is: _________________________________  
Stress level, **0 to 10:** ______

### 2. Notice it
One body sign or behavior I notice is: ________________________  
Examples: tight jaw, headache, fast thoughts, trouble sleeping

### 3. Find the trigger
The situation, thought, or worry behind this stress is:  
__________________________________________________________

### 4. Pause
I will take three slow breaths and remind myself:  
“_______________________________________________________”

### 5. Choose one safe action
For the next 10 minutes, I will:  
☐ walk or stretch  ☐ write  ☐ take a quiet break  
☐ call or text __________________  ☐ leave the situation  
☐ do another safe activity: ________________________________

### 6. Check the result
My stress level now, **0 to 10:** ______  
One thing that helped was: __________________________________

**Reminder:** Strong emotions are warning signs, not orders. If you feel close to using, move away from substances and contact a trusted person or local emergency services.

**Source:**  
[1] Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration, *Matrix Intensive Outpatient Treatment for People With Stimulant Use Disorders*, RP 20, “Recognizing Stress”; RP 18, “Emotional Triggers”; ERS 7A, “Thoughts, Emotions, and Behavior.”
