User Prompt:
Create a one-page worksheet-style recovery activity for someone trying to manage intense emotions without using substances and they are often stressed out. The activity should include 4 to 6 brief fill-in prompts. Responses should be short enough to complete directly on a handout.

Output:
# Stress Pause: Choose, Don’t Use

Stress can show up in your body, thoughts, and actions. Noticing early warning signs gives you a chance to pause and choose a safer response. Use this page when emotions feel intense. [1]

**Date:** ____________________  **Time:** ____________________

### 1. Name the emotion
I feel mostly: ______________________________________________  
Intensity, **0 to 10:** ______

### 2. Notice my warning sign
In my body, thoughts, or behavior, I notice:  
__________________________________________________________

### 3. Identify the stress
The main problem or trigger is:  
__________________________________________________________

### 4. Separate what I can control
One part I can control is: ___________________________________  
One part I cannot control is: _________________________________

### 5. Choose a recovery action
For the next 10 minutes, I will:  
☐ take three slow breaths  ☐ walk or stretch  
☐ write one sentence  ☐ call/text __________________  
☐ leave the situation  ☐ do: _______________________________

### 6. Check in
My emotion level now, **0 to 10:** ______  
The action helped by: ______________________________________

**Reminder:** Feelings are real, but they do not have to control your behavior. If you feel close to using, move away from substances and contact a trusted person or local emergency services.

**Source:**  
[1] Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration, *Matrix Intensive Outpatient Treatment for People With Stimulant Use Disorders*, RP 20, “Recognizing Stress”; RP 18, “Emotional Triggers”; RP 22, “Reducing Stress.”
