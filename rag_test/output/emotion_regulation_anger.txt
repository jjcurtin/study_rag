User Prompt:
Create a one-page worksheet-style recovery activity for someone trying to manage intense emotions without using substances and they are often angry. The activity should include 2 to 4 brief fill-in prompts. Responses should be short enough to complete directly on a handout.

Output:
# Anger Pause Plan

Anger is a real feeling, not a command. In recovery, noticing anger early can help you choose a safe response instead of using substances. Take a short pause before acting. [1]

**Date:** ____________________  **Time:** ____________________

### 1. Notice the warning signs
My body or behavior tells me I am getting angry when:  
__________________________________________________________

### 2. Name the trigger
The situation or thought that started this anger is:  
__________________________________________________________

### 3. Choose my pause
Before I speak or act, I will:  
☐ take three slow breaths  ☐ step away  ☐ walk or stretch  
☐ write one sentence  ☐ call _______________________________

### 4. Use a safer response
What I want to say or do is: _________________________________  
A calmer, recovery-supporting choice is: _______________________

**Reminder:** If you might hurt yourself or someone else, leave the situation and contact local emergency services. Do not use substances to manage anger.

**Source:**  
[1] Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration, *Matrix Intensive Outpatient Treatment for People With Stimulant Use Disorders*, RP 23, “Managing Anger”; RP 18, “Emotional Triggers.”
